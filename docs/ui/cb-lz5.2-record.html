cb-lz5.2 — how what you agreed gets written down, and what the designer sees of it

The five headings are settled (cb-lz5.1): ## The agreed experience, ## The states, ## The words, exactly, ## What was considered and rejected, ## The mockup. What is not settled is whether the designer reads that record and approves it before it is written, or reads it after it has been. That is the question on this page.

What is being written, in both options

This is what a developer opens, days later, having never met the designer. It is the same document in both options; only when the designer sees it differs.

## The agreed experience

The list pane grows a Rows control at the right of its header, showing the current
limit as a number. Clicking it opens a short menu — 50, 100, 250, 500, All — and choosing
one reloads the list at once. The chosen limit is remembered for the next visit.

## The states

Empty list     the control still shows, dimmed, reading `Rows 100'.
Still loading  the control is dimmed and does not open.
Fewer rows     than the limit: the control reads `Rows 100', unchanged.
Narrow window  the control drops to the line below the title.

## The words, exactly

The control reads Rows. The menu entries read 50, 100, 250, 500, All.
An empty list reads Nothing to show yet.

## What was considered and rejected

A slider — rejected: it invites values nobody wants and gives no way to say "All".
A setting in preferences — rejected: the limit is changed while looking at the list,
not before opening it.
Remembering per list — rejected: one remembered number, everywhere, was preferred as
being easier to predict.
