cb-0q1 — a question waits until it is answered

Two interview rounds, 2026-09-12. This file is the specification for what the navigator sees once a question from a session no longer expires. Where this file and the bead's plan disagree, this file is right.

Today a session that asks the navigator something gives up: after fifteen minutes (implementer) or thirty (interactive role) a [cerebro] line is typed into it, and the role instructions turn that into "write down where you got to, park the work for a person, end the pass". That whole path goes. A question now waits, and the session waits with it.

1. A long-unanswered question looks exactly as it does now chosen — nothing changes

The row stays bold, with a gold ? and its clock counting up, for as long as it takes. Two hours and two minutes look alike except for the number, and that was accepted: the row is already the only bold one on the screen, and nothing new is worth a new thing to learn.

AGENT        ROLE         STATE     BEAD        FOR
● Rogue        implementer  working   cb-9k2      41m  12m
? Xavier       ux           asking    cb-0q1      2h14 2h14
● Beast        ux           working   cb-4tt      18m  18m
? Moira        user-feedbk  asking     —          8m   8m
● Gambit       build-desgn  working   cb-7h1      6m   6m

Rejected: a gold waiting 2h14 phrase replacing the clock past an hour — one more thing a row can say, and an hour is a number somebody has to pick. Rejected: a standing 2 questions waiting for you — longest 2h14 count in the header — the top line is where every passing message appears, and a standing count competes with them.

Nothing is typed into an asking session by the clock, at any elapsed time, for any role. No message, no nudge, no hand-back, no end of pass, no retirement. When the navigator answers — a minute later or four hours later — the session carries on from the answer.

2. The two self-reports stop measuring waiting chosen

Health and History both point at things taking unusually long. A session waiting on the navigator is not the fleet running slowly, so neither counts it.

Xavier has been asking for 134m. What the lower half of the screen shows:

History 2
  Rogue working 41m, median 35m
  Gambit working 6m, median 9m

— Health reports nothing at all; there is nothing wrong.

What it must NOT show:
Health 1
  Xavier has been asking for 134m — much longer than usual
History 3
  Xavier asking 134m — long, median 2m

A waiting session leaves Health silent and is absent from History entirely, rather than present-but-not-gold: the time it spent waiting is the navigator's, and averaging it into what the fleet takes makes every later reading wrong too.

3. Nothing is parked by a clock chosen

The "needs a person" list no longer fills up with work a session gave up on. The sessions stay open instead, each holding its work and its question.

an afternoon away from the desk — after:
Needs a person  0

? Xavier       ux           asking    cb-0q1      4h02 4h02
? Beast        ux           asking    cb-4tt      2h11 2h11

— and not, as today:
Needs a person  2
  cb-0q1  … — parked 4h ago
  cb-4tt  … — parked 2h ago
◌ Xavier       ux           standby
◌ Beast        ux           standby

The cost was named and accepted: several sessions can sit occupied all afternoon rather than freeing themselves for other work. What is bought is that answering one resumes the conversation exactly where it stopped, instead of a written summary somebody starts again from.

A session may still park work for a person for its own reasons — because the question is genuinely one nobody present can answer. What is gone is parking because time passed.

4. The other clock stays, and its gap is closed chosen — close the gap

A separate clock ends a session that has gone quiet mid-work — a wedged or crashed one. That stays: without it such a session sits there for ever.

But a session that asks its question as ordinary text, rather than through the question box, looks exactly like a wedged one — its row says working and it has stopped. Today it is woken, then ended, and the question dies with it.

what must NOT happen:
✗ Beast        ux           working   cb-4tt      stuck 30m
[cerebro] Your turn ended while your state file still said you were working…
◌ Beast        ux           standby
header: Beast was stuck and did not answer; its session was ended.

what happens instead — it marked itself as waiting when it asked, however it asked:
? Beast        ux           asking    cb-4tt      2h11 2h11

Every session marks itself as waiting whenever it puts a question to the navigator — through the question box or in plain text. Invisible to the navigator; it is a rule the sessions follow. The visible promise is the one it buys: no question put to the navigator is ever ended by a clock, however it was asked.
