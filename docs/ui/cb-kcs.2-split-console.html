The split console, as chosen

This is the screen cb-kcs.2 builds, agreed with the navigator over three rounds on 2026-09-01. It refines docs/ui/cb-kcs-supervisor.html, which settled the shape — a persistent split console, follow-selection, a stacked narrow layout, quit refused over live agents and 10 000 retained scrollback lines — and it settles what that mockup left open, all of which arrived with cb-42k giving Fleet and Work independent focus and scrolling.

  • Tab visits Fleet → Work → Session; Shift-Tab reverses.
  • In Fleet, ↑/↓ move the selection and the pane scrolls to keep it visible; PgUp/PgDn move it a page.
  • A fixed 40-cell left column holds Fleet over Work; the Session pane takes every remaining cell. Below 100 cells all three stack.
  • Only k asks before acting.
  • A focused session receives every key, Esc and Ctrl-C included; Shift-Tab is held back and is the only way out.
