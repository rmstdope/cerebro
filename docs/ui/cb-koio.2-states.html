The states of the Time tab

Tiles with a grey band for waiting on you, as agreed. Sections 1–6 are what I propose. Sections 7 and 8 are choices for you.

1 · Pointing at a tile (or a long press on a phone)

Exact numbers appear in a small box. A click, or Enter, goes one level down. The path at the top goes back up, and so do Backspace and Esc.

6 · What it remembers, and new time arriving

The period and the level you are on (for example producer › Cyclops) survive a reload, and leaving the tab and coming back. While you watch, the numbers update every minute. Tiles grow in place and never move you to another level. An agent no longer in the fleet keeps its tile for the time it ran. A long name is cut short with … and shown in full in the box from section 1. Each phase keeps the same colour on every agent, so 'build' is always blue.
