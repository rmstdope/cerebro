Fleet — shown when the Fleet tab is selected

Loading, empty, unavailable, and older information

Loading says “Loading fleet…” or “Loading work…” in the selected tab. An empty place says “No agents are in the fleet yet.” or “No work is in this column.” If one tab is unavailable, the other stays available and the failed one says “Couldn’t load fleet. Try refreshing this page.” or “Couldn’t load work. Try refreshing this page.” After a failed update, the last successful information remains with “Showing information from 2 minutes ago. Refresh to try again.”

New information updates the open tab and any open detail popup automatically. If the selected item disappears, its popup closes and focus returns to the card or row that opened it. “Refresh” immediately tries again and says “Refreshing…” while it runs. The selected tab is remembered after a reload; an open detail view is not. At a narrow width, the board scrolls sideways one lane at a time and the attention area wraps; all controls remain reachable by keyboard.
