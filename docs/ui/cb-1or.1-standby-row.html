cb-1or.1 — an implementer between beads: the same path a role takes

Same fleet in every panel; only the Cyclops row differs. Today an implementer that finishes writes done (green ◍ for a tick) and is replaced at once, and one with nothing to claim sits idle polling for ever. After this bead it writes waiting (◐, as a planner does), is ended half a minute later, its buffer kept, and reads standby (◌) until a planned bead exists — then the view starts a fresh session, at most one implementer every 30s. What differs between the options is the words in the For column, the echo line and the RET text.

◌ Cyclops        implementer   standby               (For column: question 1)

Also this row when the implementer started and found nothing to claim: it writes waiting at once rather than polling.
