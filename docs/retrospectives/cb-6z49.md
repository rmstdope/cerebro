# cb-6z49

## A branch-against-main A/B of the loaded local gate pointed at a diff that was innocent

**What happened.** `bash tests/gate` was red on `tests/fleet-keys.sh` ("timed out waiting for: the stub agent to start"), and on some runs also on `fleet-producer-start`, `fleet-launch`, `fleet-board` and `fleet-type`. The load average was 6 to 9, with other fleet sessions running. To check whether the diff was the cause, I ran the full bash half with main's versions of the four changed files swapped in. Main was green 3 out of 3; the branch was red 6 out of 7. Bisecting down to `scripts/roster` alone kept it red. Then fleet-keys passed alone, and passed beside the other fleet suites with the branch's `roster`, and an instrumented run caught `launch` four seconds into a trivial `agent-cli --default-tool`. CI on ubuntu was green on both heads, fleet-keys included. The diff touched no code path `launch` takes.

**Why.** Not established beyond load. The samples ran a few minutes apart under load that varied between them (the per-suite durations of the paired runs differed by about 30% across *every* suite, not only the ones that failed), so a handful of paired runs could not separate the diff from the machine.

**Cost.** About 40 minutes and eight full bash-half runs, on a bead whose change was ready.

**Prevent by.** Adding a line to `skills/produce-bead`, *Building*: when only pty-driven `tests/fleet-*.sh` suites fail locally on timeouts, and the one-minute load average is near the core count, push and let CI decide. A local branch-against-main A/B is evidence only when the samples are interleaved under the same load, and the per-suite durations in the two runs' suite-logs show whether they were.

**Seen before.** cb-lt16, cb-8484, cb-7jmm, cb-gyw0 and cb-eguq record the same red-locally, green-in-CI pty suites under load. None records an A/B against main misleading the diagnosis.
