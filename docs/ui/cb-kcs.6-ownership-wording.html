The ownership wording, as chosen

What cb-kcs.6 ships. Two decisions: the sentences the previous bead's implementer chose are ratified verbatim, and an Emacs reconciliation that signalled stops borrowing the lock-error sentence and gets its own reason word. It is the Emacs twin of Ratatui's fleet_supervisor could not be read, which exists for exactly the same reason — a view that failed to work out ownership may still be holding the lease, so it must not claim nobody holds it.
